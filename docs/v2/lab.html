Where the name goesA/B

The brief offered bottom-left or top-centre. Bottom-left for three reasons. The 608 rows with no screenshot fall back to artwork that is centred and top-weighted, and the other candidate fallback — GitHub’s social card — writes the repository name in the upper middle of the frame, so a top-centre overlay lands on top of the one thing already written there. Bottom-left shares a left edge with the metadata row underneath, which makes the card one column of text instead of two competing alignments. And a scrim that darkens upward from the bottom never covers the subject of a screenshot, which is almost always the top two-thirds of a UI.
