How lookalikes are found

Every project in the atlas has two short fingerprints of meaning: one of its README, one of the sentence describing it. A lookalike is close on both, shares the project’s category or the harnesses it is built for, and shares the less common words in its description. Projects from the same maintainer, or named after it, are listed separately, because an add-on for something is not an alternative to it.

For a project the atlas doesn’t list, your browser asks GitHub or GitLab for its description, topics and README, and compares those instead. GitHub allows 60 of those lookups an hour from one connection. Nothing you type is sent anywhere else.
