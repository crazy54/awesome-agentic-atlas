How this is made

A weekly read of the Hugging Face Hub's public API. A model is kept when it is popular in its own right or can be run locally from a trusted re-packaging, and every re-packaging of it — from its own lab, or from bartowski, unsloth, lmstudio-community, ggml-org, mlx-community, RedHatAI or hugging-quants — is one entry under it rather than a model of its own. Sizes are the files' own byte counts, split parts summed, in decimal gigabytes. NSFW-tagged repos, adapters, empty cards, small merges and long-abandoned forks are left out. The whole index is one JSON file, documented in the handbook.

Licences are as each model's card states them. Custom terms means the model has its own licence (Llama, Gemma, OpenRAIL and others) that you should read before shipping it; gated means the original repository asks you to accept that licence first.
