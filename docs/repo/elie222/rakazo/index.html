Rakazo

elie222/rakazo

Self-hosted platform for persistent AI teammates with their own conversations, memory, and routines, running on shared team computers or isolated private ones, reachable from web, desktop, and mobile with voice mode. Bots delegate to peer bots or short-lived subagents. BYO model and sandbox.

TypeScript Apache-2.0
